Xenakis UPIC - installation

VST3 (Linux)
------------
1. Copy the "Xenakis UPIC.vst3" folder into ~/.vst3/.
2. Rescan plugins in your host (YAWN, Bitwig, Reaper, ...).
3. Add "Xenakis UPIC" to an instrument track.
4. Open the editor, press Open, and choose a .xenpage file from presets/,
   or drag-edit a new page and press Play.

Standalone (Linux)
------------------
1. Extract xenakis-upic-studio-<version>-linux-x86_64.tar.gz.
2. Run "./Xenakis UPIC Studio".
3. Optionally pass a preset:
   "./Xenakis UPIC Studio" presets/03-gendyn-drone.xenpage

Quick controls
--------------
- Draw: drag from the start of an arc to its end.
- Erase / Select: choose the tool in the toolbar.
- Play scans the page; Loop, Reverse and Speed are in the transport.
- The side tabs generate pages with Sieve, Markov, Cloud, Game and GENDYN.
- Ctrl/Cmd + mouse wheel zooms; mouse wheel scrolls.
