UPIC Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

UPIC Studio is an offline music composition app published by the UPIC Studio project. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data collection

UPIC Studio does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no network permission. It does not request the microphone, location, contacts, camera, or any other runtime permission.

Files and local programs

UPIC Studio reads and writes .xenpage files only when you explicitly choose a file through Android's system document picker. Those files remain under your control and are not uploaded anywhere.

The app also keeps the current page, plus any programs you save in the program library, in its private app storage on your device. This local data never leaves the device. It is removed when you uninstall the app; individual saved programs can be deleted from the Programs dialog.

Diagnostics

UPIC Studio does not collect crash reports or usage telemetry. If you contact the project with a bug report, any information you voluntarily include is used only to investigate that report.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be published at this location with a new effective date.
